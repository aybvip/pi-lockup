π🔒 What a Pi lockup actually is

A lockup buys a rate, not a return — and it changes a π's state, not its identity. Everything below is either quoted from an official document or measured on the public ledger.

The official formula

L(B) = Lt · Lp · log10(N) · B

Pi Whitepaper, December 2021 — Token Model and Mining

Why it is counter-intuitive

+559.90 % is one number with three unknowns. Multiple (Lt, Lp, N) combinations reproduce it, so the displayed boost does not reveal the settings.

The year constant is a Julian year — 365 × 86 400 would be 31 536 000, which is 20 952 seconds too short. Locked π is still "asset": "native": the lock is a time predicate, not another token.

Two lockups, easily confused

A · mining-app (setting)

A percentage + duration stored as an account setting. Nothing is locked until your next migration to Mainnet. Editing it affects only the next transfer.

B · wallet (state)

Locks π immediately. Boost starts with the next mining session. It explicitly does not change the app setting used for the next migration.

The PiPower rule — quoted, not paraphrased

“Any user with an active Pi lockup of ≥90% of their mined tokens for at least 3 years at the time of a token launch created before February 20, 2026 automatically receives a standardized, platform-defined baseline PiPower … The number for PiPower_Baseline will be calibrated.”

— PiRC1 §3

A participation capacity, not free π. No published value. And the date clause is genuinely ambiguous — this project refuses to resolve it by assertion.
